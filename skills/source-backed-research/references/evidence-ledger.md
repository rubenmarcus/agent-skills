# Evidence ledger

| Claim | Status | Original source | Source date / observation time | Scope and sample | Counterevidence or limitation | Decision affected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Statuses:

- **Observed:** directly inspected output, behavior, or artifact; retain reproduction details.
- **Source-reported:** attributed to a source, with its measurement or reporting limits.
- **Inferred:** reasoning from named observations; explain the inference.
- **Hypothesized:** a proposed explanation with a discriminating test.
- **Contradicted:** identify the conflicting evidence and what it rules out.
- **Unverified:** missing access, incomplete measurement, or an unresolved source claim.

For social research, retain original context and explain how examples were selected. Do not extrapolate engagement into revenue or distinct participants. A useful experiment names the audience, intervention, measured outcome, attribution window, and what would change the recommendation.
